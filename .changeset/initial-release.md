Initial release of `@peerbots/face` (`0.1.0`) providing dual PixiJS v8 2D and Vizij 3D robot face renderers, canonical `FaceAssets`, accessible `StaticFaceFallback`, modular Zustand rendering and speech slices, two-tier `TtsAudioCache` (in-memory LRU + IndexedDB), and `FaceSettingsPanel` controls.
